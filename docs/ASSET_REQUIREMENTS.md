# Asset Requirements

## Current policy

Use procedural geometry, original work, or assets with documented commercial
redistribution rights. No copyrighted game assets or unverified branded vehicle
models may be imported.

## Needed for future milestones

- Modular residential, commercial, civic, and industrial buildings.
- Original Godavarikhani-style signs and storefronts.
- Cars, motorcycles, autos, buses, bicycles, and traffic props.
- Pedestrians with reusable animation sets.
- Road markings, utility poles, boundary walls, trees, and street furniture.
- Optimized mobile texture sets and LOD variants.

Every external asset must be recorded in `ASSET_LICENSES.md` before use.
