# Weapons: lessons

Code: `scripts/gameplay/weapon_catalog.gd` (every rifle's model, grips, muzzle and stats),
`person_controller.gd` (holding and aiming), `city_gameplay.gd` (firing, ownership, switching),
`weapon_pickup.gd`, `tdm_session.gd` (host checks).

## Adding a new rifle

1. Export it with a script like `tools/export_ak47.py`: metres, barrel along +Z, origin at the pistol grip,
   textures embedded, loose parts removed or listed.
2. Add an entry to `weapon_catalog.gd` with a **new id** (never renumber; ids go over the network). Line its
   bore up with the M416's (x 0.097, y 0.109) through `offset`, so aiming and sights keep working, and give its
   own hand grips and muzzle in the holder's space.
3. Measure the model (grip, handguard, muzzle) rather than guessing; render the character holding it.
4. Keep the head damage within the host's `max_hit_damage` (100) and `min_claim_interval` below the fire interval.
5. Bump `PROTOCOL_VERSION` in `tdm_session.gd` if stats or messages change, and add the licence to
   `ASSET_LICENSES.md`.
6. Run `weapon_switch_regression`, `weapon_pickup_regression`, `codex_weapon_presentation_regression`,
   `tdm_match_regression`, `tdm_network_regression`, `tdm_integration_regression`.

## Entries

### Every shot built a new mesh and material, and all rifles sounded alike (2026-10-30)
- **Symptom:** one 6 KB `shot.wav` for every rifle at a fixed pitch, and each round allocated a tracer and a flash.
- **Cause:** `_tracer()` and `_shot_flash()` in `city_gameplay.gd` created nodes per shot.
- **Rule:** gun presentation lives in `gun_feel.gd`, uses pools built once and never changes damage or claims. A new
  rifle needs a line in `SOUND_NAMES` and takes from `tools/generate_gun_audio.py`. Decals are not available in the
  Compatibility renderer, so bullet marks are pooled quads. Keep a Team Deathmatch hit's blood and sound until the
  host confirms it, or a refused hit bleeds.
- **Check:** `gun_feel_regression` (300 shots add no nodes, no take plays twice running, tails fit the voice pool).

### One key doing two jobs (2026-10-30)
- **Symptom:** R reloaded and also snapped the player back up to 14 m ("Returned to safe ground"); E peeked right and
  also opened shops; B teleported to the bridge, so its fire-mode toggle never ran.
- **Cause:** `main.gd` read `vehicle_recover` on foot, `lean_right` shares E with `interact`, and `main._input` consumed B.
- **Rule:** one key, one job per control state. R reloads on foot (recovery is Home), lean needs a rifle out and
  aiming blocks E interaction, X switches fire mode, B is look-behind only, F9 is the bridge.
- **Check:** `tests/match_input_regression.gd`.

### Street rifles and saves reached into a match (2026-10-30)
- **Symptom:** walking over a pickup in Team Deathmatch set a 30-round magazine to 24, and autosave could write match
  gear (all rifles, 9999 reserve) into the free-roam save.
- **Rule:** while `gameplay.tdm_active`, pickups do nothing and `save_progress` returns false. Pickups fill to
  `gameplay.mag_size`, never a fixed number.
- **Check:** `tests/match_input_regression.gd`.
### Shot feedback lived in throwaway nodes and fixed recoil (2026-10-30)
- **Symptom:** every shot went dead centre, with no impact and no hit sound; recoil was random sideways only.
- **Cause:** `attack()` built new meshes per shot and had no spread or pattern.
- **Rule:** per-rifle `spread`, `bloom` and `recoil_pattern` live in `weapon_catalog.gd`; these are local aim only and
  need no `PROTOCOL_VERSION` bump. Damage and fire rate did not change. Visuals go through the pooled
  `shot_effects.gd`. Keep a still first shot under 0.6 degrees, so it lands on a body at 10 m.
- **Check:** `feel_regression` `test_spread_and_pattern`, `test_shot_effects`; `combat_occlusion_regression`.

### New exported rifle not recognized without editor reimport (2026-10-30)
- **Symptom:** `No loader found for resource: res://assets/models/weapons/bren2.glb` during regression tests.
- **Cause:** Godot's resource loader requires `.godot/imported/` cache entries generated by the editor before runtime `load()` succeeds on raw `.glb` files.
- **Rule:** Always run `godot --headless --path . --editor --quit` after exporting a new weapon model to ensure Godot processes and imports the file.
- **Check:** `weapon_switch_regression` verifies that `Weapons.build(id)` instantiates the model without errors.

### Loose pieces floating under the M416 (2026-10-25)
- **Cause:** the source model carries three accessory meshes not attached to the rifle.
- **Rule:** measure each part of a downloaded weapon; hide or delete pieces that are not part of it
  (`loose_parts` in the catalog).

### Rifle stats were scattered constants (2026-10-29)
- **Cause:** damage, fire rate, recoil, muzzle and grips were hard-coded for the M416 in five files.
- **Rule:** one source of truth, `weapon_catalog.gd`. The host takes damage and the fire-rate limit from the rifle
  named in the claim and refuses unknown ids, so a client cannot claim more than a real rifle does.

### Assigning a plain array to a typed one (2026-10-29)
- **Symptom:** `Invalid assignment ... owned_rifles with value of type 'Array'`.
- **Rule:** `owned_rifles` is `Array[int]`; use `give_rifle()` or a typed array, not `[0, 1]`.

### New touch button overlapped others on tablets (2026-10-29)
- **Cause:** the ⇄ swap slot was placed beside the grenade without checking every device shape and scale.
- **Rule:** any new touch slot goes in `claw_layout.gd` + `button_slots()` + `hud_layout_profiles.gd` and must pass
  `mobile_controls_regression` (7 device shapes × 3 scales × both hands).
