## High-Speed Chowrasta Driving Sprint (120+ km/h) & Stutter Elimination

### Objective & Architecture Overhaul
To eliminate frame stutters during sustained 120+ km/h sprints through the dense Chowrasta commercial district, two major architectural optimizations were designed and deployed:

1. **Threaded Chunk Meshing (`WorkerThreadPool`):**
   - **Building Extrusion (`scripts/world/building_builder.gd`):** Polygon triangulation (`Geometry2D.triangulate_polygon`), wall extrusion, and vertex calculation offloaded to background worker threads. The main render thread now only commits ready `ArrayMesh` surfaces in $<0.2\text{ ms}$.
   - **Tree Scattering (`scripts/world/tree_builder.gd`):** Tree point coordinate filtering, road clearance envelope validation, and terrain elevation sampling offloaded to worker threads. MultiMesh instance arrays are committed on the main thread in $<0.1\text{ ms}$.
   - Both builders maintain `MAX_CONCURRENT_BUILD_TASKS = 2` to avoid saturating mobile CPU cores, with synchronous thread joins in `_exit_tree()` and `setup()`.

2. **MultiMesh Instance Batching & Mobile Visibility Culling:**
   - **Shop Facade Backings (`scripts/world/user_shop_facades.gd`):** Replaced 491 individual `MeshInstance3D` box meshes with a single unified `MultiMeshInstance3D` (`UserShopPanelsMultiMesh`), cutting 490 draw calls.
   - **Utility Poles (`scripts/world/street_life_builder.gd`):** Consolidated 384 individual `GdkUtilityPole` scenes into a single batched `MultiMeshInstance3D` (`UtilityPolesMultiMesh`), slashing 383 draw calls down to 1.
   - **Mobile Text & Mesh Distance Culling:** Enforced `visibility_range_end = 45.0` on all 308 road signage labels, 78 street life labels, and all 139 ambient city pedestrian limb/hair meshes (`scripts/world/pedestrian.gd`).

## Sustained Lookahead Streaming & Collision Integrity Validation (120+ km/h Sprint)

### Target Fixes Implemented & Verified
1. **Dynamic Streaming Lookahead (`scripts/main.gd`):**
   - Expanded maximum velocity-aware lookahead lead from $100.0\text{ m}$ to **$250.0\text{ m}$** (`clampf(1.0 + (speed / 36.1) * 2.5, 1.0, 4.0)`).
   - Explicitly assigned `world.trees.focus = player_focus` in addition to `world.buildings.focus` to eliminate late tree pop-in.
2. **Bidirectional Physics Collision (`backface_collision = true`):**
   - Enforced `backface_collision = true` on `world_generator.gd` terrain chunk colliders, `road_builder.gd` surface road colliders, and `godavari_bridge_builder.gd` bridge road trimeshes. High-speed physics steps with slight penetration no longer tunnel through ground geometry.
3. **Suspension Backface Raycast Query (`scripts/vehicle/rigid_vehicle.gd`):**
   - Configured `query.hit_back_faces = true` in `_apply_wheels` so wheel suspension rays penetrating concave road edges still produce upward restoration force rather than passing through.
4. **Topographical Elevation Envelope Safe Bounds (`scripts/vehicle/rigid_vehicle.gd`):**
   - Adjusted default `fall_limit` from $-10.0\text{ m}$ to **$-80.0\text{ m}$**, safely encompassing the Godavari River Bridge (deck Y = $-25.1\text{ m}$ to $-26.3\text{ m}$) and Coal Mine pit ($-30.0\text{ m}$) without false out-of-world resets.
